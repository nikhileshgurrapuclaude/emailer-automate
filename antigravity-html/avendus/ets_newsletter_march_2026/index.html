In our latest edition of the ET&S Newsletter, we bring you a comprehensive view of the key trends shaping the IT and ITeS landscape.

The sector’s performance is increasingly being driven by long-term structural outlook rather than near-term earnings, with client engagement models steadily shifting toward advisory and implementation-led work, even as traditional managed services continue to face revenue deflation. In parallel, Generative AI is reshaping delivery into a “Human + Token” operating model - augmenting productivity, driving workforce reskilling, and redefining cost structures across service delivery. This transformation is also reflected in evolving commercial constructs, with a clear shift away from traditional Time & Materials (T&M) toward outcome-based, fixed-fee, and non-FTE subscription-led pricing models.

While the prevalence of multi-year outsourcing contracts continues to moderate the pace of legacy system replacement, GenAI capabilities are now increasingly embedded across RFPs and contract renewals. Enterprises are actively undertaking cost optimization and vendor consolidation initiatives within traditional IT services to fund incremental investments in data modernization and AI infrastructure. The market has now transitioned from experimentation to scaled, production-grade deployments, with Agentic AI emerging as a key theme - enabling autonomous, multi-step execution across enterprise workflows.

Token consumption is also emerging as a meaningful cost lever within GenAI deployments, prompting enterprises to actively optimize usage and automation levels to balance efficiency with cost discipline. This is being driven by the increasing complexity and compute intensity of large language models, which are enabling multi-layered, domain-specific agent architectures across sectors such as BFSI, engineering, and broader enterprise functions.

The rapid acceleration in GenAI adoption is also driving significant demand for underlying compute infrastructure, creating strong momentum within the semiconductor ecosystem. With global semiconductor revenues expected to approach USD 1 tn by 2030, semiconductor ER&D outsourcing is emerging as a key growth segment, projected to grow at ~17% CAGR, supported by rising chip design complexity and a global shortage of specialized engineering talent. The addressable market is also expanding, with system companies - including hyperscalers, automotive OEMs, and industrial players - investing in in-house silicon capabilities.

The quarter was also characterized by continued consolidation and platform creation across semiconductor engineering services, as well as AI and analytics segments, reflecting sustained M&A momentum in high-value areas of technology services. Notable transactions included IBM’s acquisition of Confluent (~USD 11 bn), Coforge’s all-stock acquisition of Encora (~USD 2.3 bn), and Tata Consultancy Services’ acquisition of Coastal Cloud (~USD 700 mn).
