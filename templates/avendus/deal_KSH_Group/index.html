KSH Group sells 100% stake in KSH Distriparks to Delhi International Cargo Terminals for USD 35 mn

The shareholders of KSH Distriparks Private Limited (“KSH”/Company), operator of Pune’s largest Inland Container Depot (“ICD”), have sold their 100% stake in the company to Delhi International Cargo Terminals Private Limited (jointly owned by J M Baxi Group and Hapag Lloyd). The ICD serves as an integrated platform for customs clearance, storage. warehousing, and transportation services, catering to Pune’s market of over 5 Lakh TEUs per annum. Led by a seasoned management team, it has established relationships amongst some of Pune’s large industrial and manufacturing players.

Avendus Capital acted as the exclusive financial advisor to KSH and its shareholders on this transaction.

Rohit Hegde, Managing Director and Chairman, KSH Distriparks said, “We are delighted to have passed on the control of our Pune ICD to J M Baxi Group. With its partnership with global shipping major Hapag-Lloyd, extensive network across India’s ports and logistics industry, and strong presence at Mumbai Port, we believe J M Baxi Group is best placed to build on the foundation we have created. We would also like to thank our shareholders who have supported us from our early days and helped scale the business.”

Dhruv Kotak, Managing Director, J M Baxi Group said, “KSH’s Pune ICD is a valuable addition to our growing network of port terminals, CFSs, ICDs and CTO operations. This transaction strengthens our capabilities to provide a more comprehensive offering to our clients and create synergistic value for the overall business. The ICD enhances our dominance in hinterlands of western India, and we look forward to building meaningful relationships with the customers of the facility.”

Gaurav Arora, Managing Director and Head, Infrastructure & Real Assets Investment Banking at Avendus Capital said, “We are pleased to have advised KSH Distriparks Private Limited on its strategic sale to the J M Baxi Group, marking yet another successful transaction in our longstanding relationship with KSH Group. KSH Distriparks has built a strong position in Pune’s logistics ecosystem, supported by its strategic infrastructure, established customer relationships and experienced management team. The transaction reflects the continued interest in high-quality logistics platforms that are integral to India’s industrial and manufacturing supply chains, as investors increasingly recognise the value of strategically located assets.
